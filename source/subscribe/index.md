## How to subscribe

1. Copy the feed address for your preferred language above.
2. Open your RSS or Atom reader and look for “Add feed” or “Follow website”.
3. Paste the address and confirm. You can then read new posts in your reader.

Opening a feed address directly may show XML text in your browser. That is normal: it is the format your reader uses. If the copy button is unavailable, select and copy the full address manually.
