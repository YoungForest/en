## Mendeley GB/T 7714-2005

- For English references, author names were shown in all caps
- For English references with more than three authors, the Chinese "等" was used for omission instead of "et al."

[Solution](https://blog.csdn.net/yitian_z/article/details/104050142)

I configured my own reference format and am sharing it here. Its features include:

- Remove `//` from references, especially the extra separator after conference names
